About Kaotic Games
An independent studio, R&D lab, and entertainment company.
Kaotic DNA — A different move
The name draws from chaos theory and game theory: question assumptions treated as fixed, understand the system, and make a different move. We build meaningful games, tools, and entertainment that give people greater power to create.
People over profit
Leadership exists to support builders. Growth should create opportunity rather than extract from people. When the spreadsheet conflicts with the people doing the work, choose the people. Technology and AI should extend human ability, lower barriers, and reduce unwanted work—not make people unnecessary.
How Stream Sync fits
Kaotic Labs is the commercial and technical engine that funds independence. Syndicate AI is the Early Access flagship. Stream Sync is part of that ecosystem, giving creators more control and less friction. The larger goal is proving technology and entertainment can succeed without sacrificing people.
Guiding principles
Build useful tools, make honest choices, and keep human accountability at the center. Creative control stays with the people who use what we ship. Success should strengthen builders and communities—not extract from them or replace them.
